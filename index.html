Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

This document governs the collection, stewardship, and protection of personal data across the ParkEase mobile and web applications, establishing transparent principles for vehicle access, physical security, and consumer rights.

01 / Overview

Purpose & Governance

ParkEase operates an urban mobility marketplace that connects independent property owners (“Hosts”) with drivers seeking verified hourly, daily, and monthly parking facilities (“Drivers”).

We believe technology should simplify urban navigation without compromising personal autonomy or digital privacy. This policy defines the boundaries of data we process, why each point of data is strictly required, and the mechanisms through which you maintain complete sovereignty over your information.

02 / Information Stewardship

Data Collected & Purpose

We operate under a strict standard of data minimization. We only collect the minimal parameters required to safely reserve physical space, provide gatekeeper verification, and execute transactions:

Category Attributes Justification
Identity & Session Name, email address, session tokens Secure authentication and reservation confirmation delivery via Supabase.
Vehicle Garage License plate (HSRP format), make, model Authorized security clearance at residential and commercial access gates.
Foreground Location Device GPS latitude and longitude Computing real-time driving distances to nearby open parking bays.
Host Inventory Listing address, bay photographs, amenities Cataloging parking inventory and providing access guidance to verified drivers.
Verified Reviews Star rating (1–5) and written feedback Establishing marketplace trust and dynamic host scoring following checkout.
Commitment Against Commercialization
ParkEase does not sell, license, rent, or trade your personal information, driving destinations, or vehicle records with advertising intermediaries, brokers, or external analytics firms.
03 / Platform Access

Runtime Device Permissions

In conformance with Google Play Store compliance directives, runtime permissions are requested only when a corresponding in-app action is initiated by the user:

  • Foreground Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Used exclusively while the application is active on screen to populate the interactive map with nearby facilities. Location access ceases immediately when the application is placed in the background or terminated.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Invoked solely when scanning an entry pass QR code or photographing a parking stall during the host onboarding workflow.
  • Photo Library (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Permits property owners to upload pre-existing photographs of parking bays from device storage.
04 / Financial Integrity

Payment Processing & Escrow

Financial settlements on ParkEase are facilitated through Razorpay Software Private Limited, an RBI-regulated and PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment aggregator.

  • Zero Card Data Retention: Card numbers, CVV codes, and net banking credentials are input directly into Razorpay’s encrypted interface and are never transmitted to or retained by ParkEase servers.
  • Direct UPI Verification: Mobile payments conducted via Unified Payments Interface (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) are authorized through hardware-isolated banking modules utilizing 256-bit encryption.
  • Cancellation Protocol: Hourly reservations cancelled before the designated check-in window are refunded 100% to the original payment method without penalty. Monthly subscriptions may be discontinued at any time.
05 / Physical Safeguards

Gate Access & 6-Digit Verification

Physical security for property hosts and parking facilities is governed through algorithmic access control:

  • Private building instructions, gate codes, and parking stall numbers remain redacted on public listings until a booking is accepted.
  • Check-in and check-out are authorized via a unique 6-digit Entry/Exit PIN system, validating driver presence and automatically computing overstay protections.
06 / Data Sovereignty

Account & Data Deletion Rights

In adherence to Google Play Store requirements and international privacy standards, users hold the absolute right to delete their profile, history, and uploaded materials:

Autonomous In-App Deletion
1. Navigate to Profile ➔ Legal, Privacy & Account Controls.
2. Select Delete Account & Data and confirm.
3. All personal records, saved vehicles, transaction references, and listings are immediately and permanently removed from our active production cluster.

Users may also request manual erasure by contacting avsco.net@gmail.com with the subject line “Data Deletion Request”. Verification and permanent erasure are fulfilled within 48 business hours.
